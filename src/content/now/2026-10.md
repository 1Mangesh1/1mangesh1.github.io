## What I'm up to right now

The authentication and authorization platform I led at work is in production: self-hosted Keycloak with custom Java SPIs, multi-tenant SSO with IdP federation, and SpiceDB ReBAC (16 object types, 45 permissions) kept consistent with PostgreSQL through a transactional outbox and an async worker. I also hardened it with mandatory TOTP MFA and instant session revocation (OIDC back-channel logout with a Redis denylist), and added supply-chain security gates to CI.
