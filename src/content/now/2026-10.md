## What I am up to right now

The authentication and authorization platform that I led at work is in production. It uses self-hosted Keycloak with custom Java SPIs, multi-tenant SSO with IdP federation, and SpiceDB ReBAC (16 object types, 45 permissions). SpiceDB stays consistent with PostgreSQL through a transactional outbox and an async worker. I hardened the platform with mandatory TOTP MFA and instant session revocation (OIDC back-channel logout with a Redis denylist). I also added supply-chain security gates to CI.
